# Service landing (Divi 5)

Purpose, SEO notes and internal links: [the shared page recipe](../../pages/service-landing.md). This page is the
Divi 5 assembly: which Divi 5 section recipes, in what order, the heading outline they produce, and a whole-page
worked example; how to read the recipes is in the [Divi 5 recipes README](../README.md).

## Sections
| # | Section | Divi 5 recipe | Tone | Headings it adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | [Hero split](../sections/hero-split.md) | navy | `h1` |
| 2 | Trust bar | [Trust bar](../sections/trust-bar.md) | light grey | none |
| 3 | Services | [Services grid](../sections/services-grid.md) | white | `h2` + `h3` (blurbs) |
| 4 | Why it matters | [Alternating features](../sections/alternating-features.md) | white | `h3` (feature titles; it borrows the Services `h2`, so it never comes right after the hero) |
| 5 | By the numbers | [Stats counters](../sections/stats-counters.md) | light grey | `h2` (its own heading row) + `h3` (counter titles) |
| 6 | Testimonials | [Testimonials](../sections/testimonials.md) (grid) | white | `h2` |
| 7 | FAQ | [FAQ](../sections/faq.md) | white | `h2` + `h3` (questions) + the `FAQPage` JSON-LD |
| 8 | CTA band | [CTA band](../sections/cta-band.md) | navy | `h4` |

**Fit the list to the brief.** Build only the sections the brief has content for, and never invent a
testimonial, logo or statistic to fill one. With no hero photo, use [Hero centered](../sections/hero-centered.md)
(same `h1`, no image). With no logos, reviews or feature copy, leave out the trust bar, testimonials and
alternating features. The outline below still holds without them: FAQ `h3` questions, then the CTA `h4`.

## Rhythm

Navy → grey → white → white → grey → white → white → navy: the hero and the closing CTA band are the only dark
sections and bookend the page, and the two light-grey bands (trust bar, stats) break up the white run. Each section
keeps its own recipe's padding (the hero's `gvid-r6secpad01` variable, 90/60/45px for the white content sections,
70/50/40px for the grey and navy bands); don't invent a page-wide scale. On Divi 5 the rows keep their recipe's
width: the hero and the trust bar are 90% / 1200px, the others Divi's default row.

## Headings

- **One `h1`:** the hero headline. No other block may set `headingLevel` `"h1"`, including the contact form
  (whose default is `h1`) if you add one.
- **`h2`:** Services, Testimonials, FAQ. The trust bar adds no section heading. The stats counters get their own `h2` ("By the Numbers", a heading row above the counters) so their `h3` titles don't read as siblings of the Services blurbs; skip that `h2` only if the band sits directly under an existing `h2` it belongs to.
- **`h3`:** the blurb titles, the feature titles, the counter titles (`number-counter` titles render as `h3`) and
  the FAQ questions.
- **`h4`:** the CTA band's title, one below the FAQ questions just before it.

`validate.py` checks the whole page: `E_MULTIPLE_H1` for a second `h1`, `W_HEADING_SKIP` for a jump of two levels.
The alternating-features section holds its second feature twice (a desktop row and an image-first tablet/phone
row, `disabledOn`), so the rendered outline lists that `h3` twice; only one copy shows at any width.

## Assembling on Divi 5

- Copy each section's worked example (or build it from the target site's tokens), then wrap the sections in one
  `divi/placeholder` block, in the order above: `divi5_blocks.wrap_placeholder(sections)` and `render_block`
  ([page-format.md](../../../reference/divi5/page-format.md)). Every block keeps `builderVersion` =
  `site.divi_version`.
- Validate the **whole page**, not the sections one by one: only the page run sees the heading outline.
- The FAQ's JSON-LD code block stays inside its FAQ section.
- Every image `src` is a Media Library URL on the target site.

- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py page.html --tokens tokens.json` — 0 errors and no heading warnings, as a whole page (no `--fragment`); no `W5_UNKNOWN_VARIABLE`/`W5_UNKNOWN_PRESET` (`tokens.json` is the target site's own; `recipes/divi5/sample-tokens.json` is only the example's fictional brand)

- [ ] Only after that approval: `python3 scripts/publish.py draft page.html --site "$SITE" --user "$WP_USER" --title "…"` — review its `preview_url` before publishing ([publishing](../../../reference/publishing.md))
- [ ] exactly one `h1` on the page and no skipped heading level
- [ ] every section's own checklist is done: testimonials, stats, FAQ answers and trust badges all come from the client's brief, none invented; the FAQ JSON-LD matches its accordion
- [ ] every image has alt text and a Media Library URL
- [ ] contrast: every text color on its background is at least 4.5:1 (3:1 only for text of 24px, or 19px bold, and up), hover and active states included ([README §2](../README.md#contrast)): each section recipe's contrast line holds on this page, since the sections keep their own backgrounds; no orange text on white anywhere
