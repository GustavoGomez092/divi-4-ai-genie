# Local SEO location

**Use for:** a location/service-area landing page — one per city or neighborhood a multi-location
or wide-radius business serves, built to rank for "[service] in [city]" and to prove local
relevance to a visitor who searched that exact phrase. · **SEO:** the city name belongs in the
`h1` itself (not just in body copy); `FAQPage` JSON-LD should mirror the on-page FAQ questions
word-for-word so the rich result matches what the visitor actually sees; every service-area link
that has its own page should link to it, exactly as [Service area list](../sections/service-area-list.md)
already does.

## Structure

| # | Section | Recipe | Tone | Heading it contributes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | [Hero centered](../sections/hero-centered.md) | dark | `h1` — put the city in the headline itself, e.g. "Miami's Fastest Emergency Plumbers" |
| 2 | Services | [Services grid](../sections/services-grid.md) | light | `h2` + `h3` (blurbs) |
| 3 | Service areas | [Service area list](../sections/service-area-list.md) | light | `h2` |
| 4 | Testimonials (local) | [Testimonials](../sections/testimonials.md) | light | `h2` |
| 5 | FAQ (local + JSON-LD) | [FAQ](../sections/faq.md) | light | `h2` + accordion (`toggle_level="h3"`, same reason as Service landing) |
| 6 | Contact (map) | [Contact](../sections/contact.md) | light | `h2` (section heading) + `h2` (the `et_pb_contact_form`'s own `title_level`, which the recipe sets directly — a second `h2` on the same section is fine, only `h1` must be singular) |
| 7 | CTA band | [CTA band](../sections/cta-band.md) | dark | `h4` |

No hero photo here (`hero-centered` has none by design) — a location page's job is proof of local
relevance, not a product shot, so the centered hero puts the city name and eyebrow front and
center instead of splitting attention with an image.

## Rhythm

dark → light → light → light → light → light → dark, the same bookend pattern as
[Service landing](service-landing.md). Services grid still runs before Service Areas so the page's
`h3` level is established early (via the service blurbs) — everything downstream (the FAQ
accordion, in particular) can safely go one level deeper than whatever `h2` came immediately
before it.

## H1/H2 placement

- **One `h1`:** the hero headline, with the city named in it.
- **`h2`:** Services, Service Areas, Testimonials, FAQ, Contact, and the contact form's own title
  — six separate `h2`s is fine; only `h1` needs to be unique.
- **`h3`:** service blurbs and the FAQ accordion items (`toggle_level="h3"` set explicitly, since
  the module's h5 default would otherwise skip two levels after a plain `h2`).
- **`h4`:** the closing CTA band, safe because the Services section's blurbs already reached `h3`
  earlier in the page.

## Internal links

- [Service area list](../sections/service-area-list.md)'s `<ul>` links each neighborhood that has
  its own location page (e.g. Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Hialeah, Doral in the worked
  example) and leaves the rest as plain text — never invent a URL for a page that doesn't exist.
- Services grid's blurbs link to the shared service pages (not duplicated per-location), so a
  visitor comparing services doesn't land on a thin, near-duplicate page.
- The contact form and CTA band both drive toward the same single goal (get a callback), matching
  a location page's job of converting a hyper-local search, not a wider comparison shop.
