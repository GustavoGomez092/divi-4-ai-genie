# Product feature

**Use for:** a page selling one specific product or plan (not the whole business) — a named
upgrade, a maintenance plan, a bundle — where the visitor needs to see what it does, compare it to
alternatives, understand the price, and get their remaining objections answered before buying. In
this worked example the "product" is Miami Rapid Plumbing's tankless-water-heater and
camera-inspection upgrades, reusing the exact features and copy already established in
[Alternating features](../sections/alternating-features.md) rather than inventing a new product
line. · **SEO:** exactly one `h1` from the hero; because Alternating Features is the very next
section (with no Services-grid `h2` in front of it to establish the outline first, unlike
[Service landing](service-landing.md)), this assembly adds a plain `h2` heading row above it —
see below.

## Structure

| # | Section | Recipe | Tone | Heading it contributes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | [Hero background image](../sections/hero-background-image.md) | dark (photo + gradient) | `h1` |
| 2 | Features | [Alternating features](../sections/alternating-features.md), adapted | light | `h2` (added) + `h3` (feature titles) |
| 3 | Tabs | [Tabs](../sections/tabs.md) | light | `h2` |
| 4 | Pricing | [Pricing](../sections/pricing.md) | light | `h2` + `h3` (table headers) |
| 5 | FAQ | [FAQ](../sections/faq.md) | light | `h2` + accordion (`toggle_level="h3"`) |
| 6 | CTA band | [CTA band](../sections/cta-band.md) | dark | `h4` |

### Adapting Alternating features with its own heading

[Alternating features](../sections/alternating-features.md)'s own worked example has no
section-level heading — it's designed to follow a section that already supplies one (as it does in
[Service landing](service-landing.md), right after Services grid). Here it's the first section
after the hero, so skip straight from `h1` to its `h3` feature titles would violate "no skipped
heading levels." Fix it the same way [README](../README.md) §3 describes for a three-up feature
row: add a full-width `et_pb_row` (one `4_4` column) carrying an `et_pb_heading` at `h2` before the
alternating rows, styled with the same `module_styles.et_pb_heading[section_tone=light,
column_type=4_4]` bundle Services grid and Testimonials already use (`title_font`,
`title_text_color`, `title_font_size` + responsive) — not a new, invented style.

## Rhythm

dark → light → light → light → light → dark. Tabs and Pricing both already carry their own `h2`
per their recipes, so once the added heading fixes Alternating Features, the rest of the page
follows the same "one `h2` per section, `h3` one level down" pattern as every other assembly here.

## H1/H2 placement

- **One `h1`:** the hero headline.
- **`h2`:** the added Features heading, Tabs, Pricing, FAQ.
- **`h3`:** the two feature titles, the three pricing table headers (`header_level="h3"`, already
  explicit in the recipe), and the FAQ accordion items (`toggle_level="h3"`, set explicitly here
  too since Pricing's `h3` headers come from a sibling section, not a guarantee the accordion's own
  default would be safe).
- **`h4`:** the closing CTA band, safe once Alternating Features/Pricing have already reached `h3`.
- Tab labels (`et_pb_tab`'s `title`) are not headings at all — Divi renders them as clickable tab
  nav items, not `h`-tags, so they don't factor into the outline.

## Internal links

- None of the sections in this assembly link off the page except the pricing tables' and CTA
  band's buttons, both `tel:` — a product page's job is to sell the one thing it's about, not to
  route the visitor back into the wider site.
