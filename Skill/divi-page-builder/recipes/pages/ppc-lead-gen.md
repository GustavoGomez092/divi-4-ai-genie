# PPC lead gen

**Use for:** the landing page a paid-search or paid-social ad points at — no navigation-heavy
sections, no menu of services to browse, one form and one message. Everything on the page either
supports filling out that form or gets out of the way. · **SEO:** irrelevant to this page's job
(it should be `noindex`d as an ad-only landing page in most setups) — what matters instead is that
the single `h1` matches the ad's promise, so nothing about "did I land in the right place" makes
the visitor bounce.

## Structure

| # | Section | Recipe | Tone | Heading it contributes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero + form | [Hero split](../sections/hero-split.md), adapted | dark | `h1` (headline) + `h2` (the `et_pb_contact_form`'s own title) |
| 2 | Trust bar | [Trust bar](../sections/trust-bar.md) | light (gray) | none |
| 3 | How it works | [Process steps](../sections/process-steps.md) | light | `h2` + `h3` (per-step titles) |
| 4 | Testimonials | [Testimonials](../sections/testimonials.md) | light | `h2` |
| 5 | CTA band | [CTA band](../sections/cta-band.md) | dark | `h3` |

### Adapting Hero split for a form instead of a photo

Keep the hero's left column exactly as [Hero split](../sections/hero-split.md) builds it (eyebrow,
`h1`, one body-copy `et_pb_text`) but **drop its button** — the form is the page's one conversion
mechanism, not a second, competing call to action. Replace the right column's `et_pb_image` with
the [Contact](../sections/contact.md) recipe's `et_pb_contact_form` (copied verbatim, fields and
all), wrapped in a column with a light `background_color` (`#ffffff`) and `border_radii` so the
form reads as a card against the hero's dark background — there is no `module_styles` bundle for
`et_pb_contact_form` in a dark hero context, so this is a from-scratch design decision, documented
here rather than invented silently. Re-word the form's own `title` for the placement (e.g. "Get
Your Free Quote" rather than "Request A Free Quote") without changing its fields, email or
success message pattern.

## Rhythm

dark → light → light → light → dark — the same bookend shape as the other assemblies, just
shorter: no Services grid, no FAQ, no pricing. Every section that would normally invite the
visitor to browse elsewhere on the site (services grid links, service-area lists, tabs) is
deliberately left out, per the "no navigation-heavy sections" rule for a PPC page.

## H1/H2 placement

- **One `h1`:** the hero headline.
- **`h2`:** the hero form's own title, and Process Steps' + Testimonials' section headings — going
  straight from `h1` to `h2` for the form title is a plain one-level step down, not a skip.
- **`h3`:** each of the four step titles in Process Steps.
- **`h3`:** the closing CTA band too: it follows the Testimonials `h2`, so `h3` is one step down
  (an `h4` there would skip a level, `W_HEADING_SKIP`) and still ranks below the section `h2`s.

## Internal links

- None inside the page body other than the form's own submit action and the CTA band's `tel:`
  button — a PPC landing page should not hand the visitor an exit before they convert. Even the
  step-by-step "How It Works" section describes the process in prose rather than linking out to a
  services page.

## Checklist
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py page.txt --tokens tokens.json` — 0 errors and no heading warnings (`tokens.json` is the target site's own, from `scripts/extract_tokens.py`; `recipes/sample-tokens.json` is only the worked example's fictional brand)
- [ ] `python3 scripts/preview.py render page.txt --tokens tokens.json --out preview.html` — fast visual check (open `preview.html`; see [preview](../../reference/preview.md))
- [ ] `python3 scripts/publish.py draft page.txt --site "$SITE" --user "$WP_USER" --title "…"` — saves a **draft** (validates first) and prints its `preview_url`; share it and publish only after the user approves ([publishing](../../reference/publishing.md))
- [ ] exactly one H1 on the page
- [ ] no skipped heading levels (`validate.py` reports any as `W_HEADING_SKIP`, and a second H1 as `E_MULTIPLE_H1`)
- [ ] the hero has exactly one call to action (the form) — no competing button
- [ ] every image has alt text and a Media Library URL
